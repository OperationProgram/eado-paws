Our Story

Started Right Here in EADO

EADO Paws is a local, independent pet care service based in the heart of East Downtown Houston. We're just getting started — which means every client gets our full, undivided attention, not a rotating cast of walkers stretched thin across the city.

Our mission is simple: provide reliable, loving care that gives you peace of mind and your pets a tail-wagging good time, every single visit.
